Depth of Reasoning: Evaluate whether the mindmap goes beyond simple lists to include causes, effects, explanations, and analytical connections.

What to look for:
1. Includes causes, effects, explanations, not just lists
2. Shows connections or logic (why/how relationships)
3. Uses examples or contrasts to show understanding
4. Some branches go beyond definitions into reasoning or implications

Score 5 (Excellent): Map shows clear lines of reasoning: causes, implications, prioritized relationships, trade-offs or examples. Demonstrates deep understanding beyond surface facts.

Score 4 (Good): Good reasoning present in many branches; a few areas are shallow. Analytical but could expand in places.

Score 3 (Acceptable): Some reasoning exists but much is descriptive rather than analytical. Basic thinking present; more depth desirable.

Score 2 (Poor): Mostly lists of items with little or no explanation or logical linkage. Superficial; lacks analytical value.

Score 1 (Very Poor): No evident reasoning; nodes are disconnected labels or bullet lists. Does not support understanding or decision-making.
