Clarity / Readability: Evaluate whether node labels are short, specific, and clear, with consistent grammar and terminology that is easy to scan.

What to look for:
1. Node labels are short, specific, and clear
2. Consistent grammar and terminology
3. Avoids jargon unless explained
4. Easy to scan — no dense or very long sentences

Score 5 (Excellent): Node text is concise, unambiguous, and consistently labeled; easy to scan. Reader immediately understands each node without guessing.

Score 4 (Good): Mostly clear; a few nodes could be phrased better or shortened. Readable overall; polish would help.

Score 3 (Acceptable): Several vague or long labels; some inconsistent terminology. Understandable with effort.

Score 2 (Poor): Many confusing or verbose labels; mixed terms and abbreviations without explanation. Reader will often be unsure of meaning.

Score 1 (Very Poor): Labels are unclear, cryptic, or contradictory. Map is not understandable.
