Usefulness: Evaluate whether the mindmap is practical for its intended use (study, teaching, planning, presentation) and can be used directly by others with minimal edits.

What to look for:
1. Practical for its intended use (study, teaching, planning, presentation)
2. Readable and logically structured enough for others to use directly
3. Combines clarity, completeness, and accuracy into actionable insight
4. Could realistically be shared or presented without major edits

Score 5 (Excellent): Immediately actionable or highly instructive; suitable for sharing or execution. High ROI — others can use it without major edits.

Score 4 (Good): Useful with minor refinement; actionable in most respects. Good for team use after small edits.

Score 3 (Acceptable): Moderately useful; requires additional detail or corrections before being operational. Starting point for work, not final deliverable.

Score 2 (Poor): Limited usefulness due to major gaps, inaccuracies, or clarity/visual problems. Not reliable for decisions.

Score 1 (Very Poor): Not useful in current form; cannot be used without substantial rework. Fails to support intended tasks.
