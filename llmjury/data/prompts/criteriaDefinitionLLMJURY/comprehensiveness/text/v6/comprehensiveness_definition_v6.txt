Coverage / Completeness: Evaluate whether all key ideas and subtopics from source material are included, with appropriate topic depth across branches.

What to look for:
1. All key ideas and subtopics from source material are included
2. No critical sections missing
3. Topic depth appropriate across branches (balanced spread)
4. Map feels like a complete overview, not fragments

Score 5 (Excellent): Comprehensive: covers primary subtopics and appropriate depth; no major gaps. Serves as a near-complete overview.

Score 4 (Good): Covers most major areas; small gaps in minor subtopics. Useful; add a few nodes for completeness.

Score 3 (Acceptable): Covers central ideas but lacks depth in important areas or misses one major subtopic. Partial; needs extension for full coverage.

Score 2 (Poor): Many missing major topics or only shallow coverage across the board. Incomplete; inadequate for decision-making or study.

Score 1 (Very Poor): Severely incomplete: critical topics absent. Not fit for purpose.
