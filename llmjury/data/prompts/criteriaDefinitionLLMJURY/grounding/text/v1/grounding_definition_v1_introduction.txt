This evaluation focuses on whether the **SUBJECT MATTER CONTENT** is grounded in sources.

**ACCEPTABLE (Not Ungrounded):**
1. **Package Metadata**:

2. **Learning Design Elements**:
   - How the package is organized or presented

   - Proficiency level descriptions
   - Package scope statements

**UNGROUNDED (Score Low):**
1. **Fabricated Subject Matter**:

Ask: "Are the TOPICS, CONCEPTS, THEORIES, and SUBJECT MATTER described in the introduction traceable to the sources?"

Sources: Basic physics textbooks (no mention of cold fusion or perpetual motion)
