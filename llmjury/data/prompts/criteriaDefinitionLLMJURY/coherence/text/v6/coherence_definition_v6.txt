Relevance: Evaluate how relevant and on-topic the information in the mindmap is, and whether child nodes conceptually belong to their parent nodes.

What to look for:
1. Every child node belongs conceptually to its parent
2. No tangents or unrelated topics
3. All examples, data, or terms make sense within the parent's context
4. Map stays focused on its central theme

Score 5 (Excellent): All nodes clearly relate to the central topic or justified subtopics. Focused with no distractions.

Score 4 (Good): Mostly relevant; a small number of nodes drift slightly off-topic but are understandable. Minor irrelevant content but core remains focused.

Score 3 (Acceptable): Some off-topic nodes or tangents that reduce cohesion; pruning suggested. Relevance mixed; reader must filter.

Score 2 (Poor): Many off-topic items or vague connections that confuse scope. Map loses topical focus; needs trimming and refocusing.

Score 1 (Very Poor): Most content is irrelevant to the central idea; map fails to represent the topic. Cannot use without major restructuring.
