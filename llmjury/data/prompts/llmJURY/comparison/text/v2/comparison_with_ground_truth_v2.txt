3. **Content Coverage**: How much of the essential information from the reference is captured?
4. **Precision**: Does the answer avoid adding incorrect or irrelevant information?
5. **Overall Fidelity**: How faithfully does the answer represent the reference content?

- **2 (Somewhat Different)**: Significant differences affecting key information or meaning
- **1 (Very Different/No Match)**: Major differences or no meaningful correspondence

**EVALUATION GUIDELINES**:
- Focus on meaning and information content rather than exact wording
- Consider both what the answer includes and what it omits
- Account for different valid ways to express the same information
- Assess whether a reader would gain equivalent understanding from both texts

**SPECIAL CASES**:
- If the answer is more concise but captures key points, consider level of information preservation
- If the answer is more detailed, assess whether additions are accurate and relevant
- Consider partial matches where only some aspects of the reference are addressed
